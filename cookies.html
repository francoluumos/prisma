What cookies are

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Similar technologies — such as your browser's local storage — work the same way. We use them to make the site work, to remember your choices, and (only with your consent) to understand and improve how the site is used.

Categories we use

Strictly necessary

Required for the site to function and cannot be switched off. This includes remembering your cookie choice itself and keeping your session working. These are set on the basis of our legitimate interest in providing the service.

Preferences

Remember choices you make — for example a configuration you were building — to improve your experience. Set only where relevant.

Analytics

Help us understand which pages are used and where the experience can be improved, in aggregate. Set only with your consent, and never used to identify you personally without a separate legal basis.

Marketing

Used to measure and, where applicable, personalise communications. Set only with your consent. We do not sell your personal data.

Managing your choice

When you first visit, a banner lets you accept all cookies or keep only the strictly necessary ones. You can change your choice at any time by clearing this site's storage in your browser settings, which will bring the banner back on your next visit. You can also block or delete cookies directly in your browser; some features may then not work as intended.
