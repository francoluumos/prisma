    Supply: shop=bicycle / bicycle repair from OpenStreetMap (ODbL), Switzerland, cleaned & de-duplicated to 1,438 shops. Demand: 2,128 municipality centroids weighted by population (Wikidata, ~8.65 M total). Coverage uses a straight-line radius as a first-pass proxy for a 15-minute drive — optimistic in the Alps, where a true road-network isochrone is the next refinement. Partner order is a greedy maximum-coverage ranking (each added partner reaches the most not-yet-covered people); centroid demand slightly inflates the very top few. Remote Alpine / Ticino / Jura communes are the expected exceptions.
